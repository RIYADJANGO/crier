## 🎓 How to Use crier

Once you have installed crier, follow these simple steps to start cross-posting:

1. **Launch the Application:** Open crier from your Applications or Programs menu.

2. **Create a New Post:** Choose the "New Post" option to start writing your content.

3. **Add Your Content:** Write or paste your content in the provided text area.

4. **Choose Your Platforms:** Select the platforms where you want to share your post. You can choose all or just a few.

5. **Customize Your Post (Optional):** Edit the content as needed for each platform. Some platforms may have different format requirements.

6. **Schedule or Publish:** Decide if you want to publish it immediately or schedule it for a later date.

7. **Click "Share":** You'll see a button to share. Click it and watch as crier does the work for you.
